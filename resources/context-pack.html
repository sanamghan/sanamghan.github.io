Build Your Personal AI Context Pack

Better AI output starts before the prompt. Give it the context that helps it understand your work, your standards, and your boundaries.

BEGINNER-FRIENDLYREUSABLE TEMPLATE

Start with five pieces.

Create a short document with your role, audience, voice, examples of strong work, and rules. This is not a life story. It is the context that changes a useful answer.

Keep this human

Only include information you have permission to share. Remove client data, passwords, personal information, and confidential strategy.

Give it a test.

Use the pack on one familiar task. Ask for a first pass, review what is generic or wrong, and add the missing context back into the document.
