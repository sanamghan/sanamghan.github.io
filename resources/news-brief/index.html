Your 15-Minute AI News Brief

Set up a daily brief around the work you actually do, delivered somewhere you will genuinely read it.

CITED SOURCESDELIVERY OPTIONSFULL SETUP

What you are building.

This is a short morning report that scans a defined set of sources and tells you what happened, what it means in practical terms, and why someone in your role should care. It can cover AI, ecommerce, beauty, real estate, healthcare, finance, marketing, or one client category.

Choose a delivery channel.

Pick the place you already check first: a Slack channel, email, workspace note, or saved file. Connect that destination through the AI tool you use, then send one test output before scheduling anything. If the automation runs on your computer, make sure it can run at the selected time.

Build a source list.

Start with five sources: one industry publication, one company blog, one community or forum, one newsletter, and one person with a point of view you trust. Specific sources produce a more useful brief than asking for “the latest news.”

Use this brief prompt.

You are generating a daily [TOPIC] news brief for [NAME] at [COMPANY].

Use these sources for news from the last 24 hours:
[SOURCE 1]
[SOURCE 2]
[SOURCE 3]
[SOURCE 4]
[SOURCE 5]

WHAT HAPPENED TODAY
Give me 5 to 10 bullets. For each: what happened, what it means in practical terms, and why someone in [ROLE] would care.

SOURCE LINKS
Include direct URLs and publication dates.

Use plain English, skip hype, say when a story is mostly noise, prioritize [FOCUS AREAS], and keep the report under 400 words excluding links. If nothing meaningful happened, say so and keep it short.

Make it yours.

Change the topic, source list, delivery, schedule, and format. A marketing manager might track digital marketing and AI tools in Slack. A beauty founder might receive beauty and DTC updates by email. A solo founder might track AI, productivity, and startup news each weekday morning.

Fix common failures.

If the brief is too long, set a harder word limit. If it misses news, name the publications and communities more specifically. If delivery fails, test the connection manually before changing the prompt. If a scheduled task misses its run, verify the task status and that the service or computer was available.

Keep this human

Use AI to find signal, not to replace source reading. Open the original source before acting on information that affects a decision.

Your next move

Run it manually for one week. Schedule it only after you know the brief is worth opening.
