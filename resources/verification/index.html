Don’t Trust It Yet

A quick review for confident AI output before it reaches a client, colleague, or public page.

VERIFICATION CHECKLISTUSE WITH ANY AI TOOL

Check five things.

Check the claim, original source, date, arithmetic, and missing point of view. If the output affects a person, commitment, or decision, a human should approve it.

The rule

An AI citation is not a source. Open the original link, read it, and make sure it supports the claim.

Ask for uncertainty.

Review your previous answer as an adversarial fact checker. List every claim that needs a source, every assumption you made, what might be outdated, and what you could not verify.
