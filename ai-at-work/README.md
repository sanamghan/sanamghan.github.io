# Hello Sanam: AI at Work mockup

This is an unpublished, editable sales-page mockup for **AI at Work: Prompts to Practical Systems**.

Open `index.html` in a browser to review it. The page uses three simple files:

- `index.html`: page structure and copy
- `styles.css`: responsive visual design
- `script.js`: interactive workflow-menu behavior

The inquiry button is intentionally a mailto draft to `sg@hellosanam.com`, and the page has no public pricing. The revenue-capacity card is illustrative: it models 15 people × 5 hours/week × 52 weeks at selectable $100/$150/$200 hourly values, and clearly does not promise revenue. Nothing here connects to or modifies the live Hello Sanam site.
